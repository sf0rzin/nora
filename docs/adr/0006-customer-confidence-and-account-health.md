# 0006 — Customer Confidence (per meeting) and Account Health (aggregated per account)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-05-07
- Deciders: NORA Team

## Context

The initial version of PROJECT.md already mentioned a **temporal Account Health Score** as an output of the Enterprise plan, but without defining how that score is calculated. During brainstorming, the idea of a **"company confidence level"** came up — refined by the team as the **CUSTOMER/lead's confidence in our company** (sentiment + buying signals/objections from the call), assessed **per meeting**.

The question was: does Customer Confidence replace Account Health, or complement it? The team agreed that it **complements**: Confidence is the per-meeting signal; Health is the account aggregate over time, fed by Confidence + risks + opportunities + recency.

## Decision

Adopt two distinct and complementary indicators for the **Enterprise** plan:

### 1. Customer Confidence Assessment (per meeting)

- Generated by the NLP worker on every analysis of a meeting with a customer/lead.
- Output integrated into `meeting-analysis-v1.schema.json`:
  ```json
  "customerConfidence": {
    "score": 62,
    "band": "MEDIUM",
    "trend": "DECLINING",
    "buyingSignals": [
      { "type": "BUDGET_DISCUSSED", "quote": "...", "weight": 0.7 }
    ],
    "objections": [
      { "type": "COMPETITOR_MENTION", "quote": "...", "severity": "HIGH", "competitor": "Senior" }
    ],
    "rationale": "..."
  }
  ```
- **Score (0–100)** combines:
  - Overall tone of the customer's speech (positive/negative, derived from `sentimentOverall` weighted by the utterances attributed to the customer).
  - Quantity and weight of **buying signals** (`BUDGET_DISCUSSED`, `STAKEHOLDER_INVOLVED`, `NEXT_STEP_REQUESTED`, etc.).
  - Penalty for **objections** (weighted by `severity`; a competitor mention with severity HIGH carries a strong penalty).
- **Band** derived: `LOW` (<40), `MEDIUM` (40–69), `HIGH` (≥70).
- **Trend** compared to the last analysis of the same `customer_account`. `null` if it is the first one.
- **Nullable**: internal meetings (with no `customer_account` linked) do not generate Confidence.

### 2. Account Health Score (aggregated per account over time)

- Snapshot recalculated whenever a new analysis generates Customer Confidence for the account.
- Persisted in `account_health_snapshots` (see data-model §2.24).
- **Score (0–100)** combines:
  - Weighted moving average (recency) of the account's last N Customer Confidence assessments.
  - Open risks on the account (penalty by `severity`).
  - Open opportunities (bonus by `estimatedValue`).
  - Recency of the last interaction (penalty for prolonged silence).
- **Band**: `AT_RISK` (<35), `WATCH` (35–59), `HEALTHY` (60–84), `STRONG` (≥85).
- **Alert trigger** when the band changes for the worse (US51).

### Data model

New tables in `docs/data-model.md` §2.19–§2.24:

- `customer_accounts` (tenant's lead/customer)
- `meeting_account_links` (N:N meeting↔account)
- `customer_confidence_assessments` (1:N per analysis; usually 1:1)
- `customer_buying_signals`, `customer_objections` (children)
- `account_health_snapshots` (time series)

### MVP scope

- Customer Confidence: **MVP (Must)**.
- Aggregated Account Health: **MVP (Should)** — simple formula based on moving average and counts; numeric refinements can evolve without a breaking change.
- Band-change alerts: **MVP (Should)** — in-app/email push; webhook is left for post-MVP.

## Consequences

**Positive:**

- Clearly defines the most valuable Enterprise output: detecting confidence erosion before churn.
- Per-meeting Confidence is easily reviewable by the AE (signals and objections come with verbatim quotes).
- Aggregated Health gives the sales manager a consolidated view without depending on the AE's memory.

**Negative:**

- Increased surface area of the worker schema — validation tests need to be updated.
- A score computed by an LLM has variance. Mitigation: low temperature, prompt with explicit criteria, periodic human validation.
- Account Health depends on `customer_accounts` being modeled — it requires minimal UI/import in the MVP (plan it as an E7 story or link it with a future CRM integration).

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Account Health only, without per-meeting Customer Confidence.** Rejected: it would lose fine-grained traceability; the AE would not know what changed in which meeting.
2. **Customer Confidence only, without aggregation.** Rejected: the sales manager needs a per-account view; computing it in the frontend on every query is expensive and unstable.
3. **OUR company's confidence in closing the deal (forecast/probability).** Rejected: this was explicitly requested — the team wants the CUSTOMER's confidence in us, not our internal probability. Forecast may be added as a separate indicator in the future.

## Accompanying Rules

- Confidence is calculated **only** when the meeting is linked to a `customer_account`; otherwise the field comes back as `null` in the schema.
- Verbatim quotes (`quote`) are mandatory in every `buyingSignal` and `objection` — without a quote, the LLM does not emit the signal.
- The Account Health snapshot is **append-only**: history is never edited; corrections become a new snapshot.
- Never use Customer Confidence in isolation to decide the AE's commission/evaluation — document this in the UI.
- The PII Shield is applied before the prompt: names of sensitive stakeholders are not sent as raw text.
